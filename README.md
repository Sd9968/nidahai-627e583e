# KABSA CALL.ai Landing

Build a modern, premium landing page for a company called KABSA CALL.ai.

Company description:

KABSA CALL.ai is an AI voice agent company that helps businesses automatically answer customer phone calls and schedule appointments 24/7. The AI voice agent can book, reschedule, modify, and cancel appointments through natural conversation. It is designed for clinics, salons, service businesses, consultants, and any business that depends on phone-based appointment booking.

The voice agent should feel fast, reliable, and human-like. Key value points:

- 24/7 appointment scheduling

- Under 500ms response latency

- English and Arabic support at launch

- More languages can be added later

- Customers can call anytime

- Appointments can be booked, modified, rescheduled, or cancelled

- After scheduling, the business or customer receives a phone notification

- Helps businesses reduce missed calls, save staff time, and improve customer experience

Design direction:

Create a webpage that feels like it was designed by a premium human designer, not a generic AI SaaS template. Avoid overused AI gradients, generic blue/purple SaaS cards, and robotic-looking sections. The design should feel clean, confident, warm, and trustworthy.

Use a color palette inspired by:

- Deep Fir: very dark green / near black background

- Sulu: fresh lime green accent

- Soft off-white / warm cream for light sections

- Muted dark green for text and borders

Suggested palette:

- Primary background: #001E17 or #00261D

- Secondary dark: #062F24

- Accent lime: #C8F25D or #D7FF70

- Soft background: #F7F6EF

- Text dark: #10241C

- Text light: #FFFFFF

- Muted text: #B8C9BF

- Border: rgba(200, 242, 93, 0.25)

Typography:

Use a premium editorial + modern SaaS typography combination.

For example:

- Headings: a classy serif or display font style

- Body: clean sans-serif

Make the hero headline feel elegant and memorable.
